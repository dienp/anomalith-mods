## Why

Players want to check pinned requirements while walking around Base and recognize the same material layout they use in the Workshop.

## What Changes

- Show the pinned checklist during active Base and Dive gameplay.
- Match the Workshop material rows, including native icons, owned/required quantities, shortage colors and OV where the cost is available.
- Keep the pinned recipe heading and compact completion status. The gameplay checklist has no pin action or blank rows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `upgrade-materials-tracking` adds Workshop-style requirements to the Base and Dive HUD.

## Impact

The tracker retains its existing pin, menu visibility setting, and Deploy material hints. Ground pickup highlights and F9 source hints remain Dive features.
