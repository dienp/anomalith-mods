## Why

The Workshop toggle sits above the materials list, but its right edge follows the inset ingredient rows. Aligning it with the Required Materials panel makes it read as part of that section.

## What Changes

- Align the right edge of both toggle states with the right edge of the native materials panel.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `upgrade-materials-tracking` specifies the Workshop toggle alignment.

## Impact

The Workshop pin view uses native panel geometry. No game assets or save data need migration.
