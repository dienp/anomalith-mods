## Why

The attachment development screen shows costs and stats without identifying which weapons can equip the selected attachment.

## What Changes

Show compatible weapons beside the selected attachment in Workshop Development and distinguish weapons that still need development.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `attachment-compatibility` displays native attachment fit in the Workshop.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

The mod reads weapon and attachment catalog data. It adds a read-only panel to the attachment development screen.
