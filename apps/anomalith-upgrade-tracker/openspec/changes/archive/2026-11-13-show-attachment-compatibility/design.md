## Context

The native Customize menu opens only supported attachment categories and marks an attachment as incompatible when the weapon's per-attachment equip scale is zero or negative.

## Decisions

Read the same category data and equip-scale check for the highlighted attachment and each revealed weapon. Use localized weapon names and the game's developed state. Show names in a compact panel along the right edge of the item preview, above Required Materials. Refresh when selection or the catalog becomes available and hide the panel for unrevealed attachments and inactive menus.
