## Why

The player wants the minimap in the bottom-left corner of the gameplay HUD.

## What Changes

- Anchor the minimap to the bottom-left corner with an inset.
- Keep its square size tied to the health bar width and retain the current zoom.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `live-minimap` updates gameplay HUD placement.

## Impact

The change applies to the standalone minimap view.
